# Technical referee report, part 1

**Scope.** `paper/sections/introduction.tex`, `regime.tex` (with Table 1, `tab:regime`), `hidden_earth.tex`
(with Table 2, `tab:inventory`, and Figure `fig:sunclock`) and `repairs.tex` (with Table 3, `tab:repairs`, and
Figures `fig:slant`, `fig:layers`). The sections were edited while this review ran (citations added, R04
shortened); line numbers refer to the files with these sha256 prefixes: introduction.tex `e156e5ec0e9c8ea2`,
regime.tex `c401ef59d55263a2`, hidden_earth.tex `8ffd5fa9ac15a572`, repairs.tex `8ce159211c368d59`,
claims.json `39352ef99a98283e`.

**Date.** 2026-10-17.

**Reviewer.** A Claude subagent acting as technical referee. Read-only except for this file. It drafted none
of the text.

**Method.** I checked every `\claim{ID}` in the four sections against its `claims.json` sources. Where a
note in this repository and Terluna's record disagreed, Terluna's record at 186b294 (`git show 186b294:<path>`)
and the pristine upstream source decided. I checked unmarked technical statements and every row of Tables
1–3 the same way. The pristine sources were ExoPlaSim 3.4.2 and SWAN 41.51, each extracted from its pinned
archive into a scratch folder, and CM1 r22.0 and the three pinned WRF-ELEC files from the research drive and
`upstream/`. On the drive I read run namelists, `progress.json`, `build.json` and WRF-ELEC's `registry.elec`.

The recomputations were all cheap:
- the formulas in `regime_calc.json`;
- the layer rms in `paper/data/layers.json`;
- the ledger totals in `twilight.json`;
- cos-latitude global means of `prw` over years 15–19 of `A28_corrected_970` and `A28_corrected_970_lw_ice`,
  and of `ps` and `lsm` over year 19 of `A28_corrected_970`, read with Terluna's GCM venv.

No model was run or built, and the test suite was not run.

**Abbreviations.**
- `terluna:` means Terluna at 186b294.
- `drive:` means `/media/projectspace/terluna-research/`.
- `pristine:` means the upstream file from the pinned archive.
- `repo:` means this repository.

**Verdicts.**
- CONFIRMED: the source says it, under the same conditions.
- WRONG: the source contradicts it.
- IMPRECISE: right in substance, but a number, condition or scope is off.
- UNSUPPORTED: nothing in the record carries it.
- MISCLASSIFIED: the form or class is wrong on the paper's own definitions.
- INCONSISTENT: two places in the paper, or the paper and its cited record, disagree.

## Most consequential problems

1. **The lunar-convection similarity is stated as fact (S12; Table 1, last row).** Terluna's record states it
   as a hypothesis. It says the microphysics and the radiation do not stretch, and the gravity pair meant to
   test it never convected. The 6 km = 1 km equivalence rests on dry similarity alone.
2. **The identity rule is broken in more places than the text admits (repairs.tex:3-7; Table 3 caption).**
   - CM1's `TERLUNA_CO2` defaults to 400 ppm and removes RRTMG's calendar-year CO₂.
   - The electrified build's NSSL gravity defaults to 9.81, where both released copies have 9.8.
   - SWAN's knee patch has no switch and matches stock SWAN only at standard gravity, not at SWAN's default
     of 9.81.

   "Five changes break the rule" and "Each default reproduces the released code" are therefore false as
   written.
3. **Classification contradicts the paper's own rule and the reach section.**
   - The gas-band fits and the curved-air tables take their values from the lunar design column, yet are
     labelled General.
   - The soil, the zeroed gases and the SWAN density are scenario or site values applied unconditionally, also
     labelled General.
   - Leakage is General while point discharge, the analogous addition, is Capability.
   - "Two classes" sits beside seven used in Table 3.
4. **H30 is misattributed.** The 58% over-absorption belongs to the sky model's 114 channels. The ledger's
   first channels failed differently, by putting near-infrared sunlight too high.
5. **R13 lists point discharge as part of the lunar runs.** It is off there (`var2 = 0`; `cm1_elec.LUNAR`), and
   Table 3 says so.
6. **"Defects that Earth hid" and the completeness of the inventory are overstated.**
   - Two of the five defects, the thread race and the lowest-level artefact, are not shown to be hidden by
     Earth.
   - The section disagrees with the class assignments of the defect log it cites (A3, A7, A8).
   - "Every instance found" omits items recorded in the repository's notes: A8, CM1's other gravity sites,
     SWAN's JONSWAP friction, and the GCM's roughness floor and σ grid.
7. **S06 is wrong.** 40% is the water's share of the surface; the share of cells is 37% (759 of 2,048).
8. **H11 and R05 misstate the ledger's baseline.** The sunlight terms are taken against the pseudo-spherical
   column and the infrared against flat layers; against all-flat columns the net is −4.2 W/m², not −5.8. The
   shells' widening (−6.5) is more than the whole net, not "nearly all" of it.
