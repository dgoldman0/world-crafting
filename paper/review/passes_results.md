- **L63–68** The electrified run's results were a string of numbers (flashes, density, start height, charge,
  hail, charge level) joined by the run as shared subject. **Revised** around two comparisons with Earth: "The
  lightning they make is rarer and larger than Earth's" (flash density a hundredth, charge per ground strike

- "What each repair moved" follows the repairs in the order they were made, each with its effect; a sequence,
  kept.
