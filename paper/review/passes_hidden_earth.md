Reviewer: Claude Opus 5.5, the drafting assistant (self-review). Date: 2026-10-17. Silent reading only.
Line numbers refer to the version before the passes' revisions.

## 1. Bead-string / equal-weight declaratives

- **Constants paragraph (L12–28)** ran model by model (CM1, NSSL, lightning, SWAN, RRTMG, beta plane), a
  chain joined by a shared subject. **Revised** around its mechanism: "A dimensional constant carries the
  scale of the world it was set on", then gravity (CM1, NSSL), Earth's air (the lightning thresholds),
  Earth's seas (SWAN's knee and density), and Earth's composition and rotation (RRTMG, the beta plane). The
  order now runs from the constant every model shares to the dormant ones, and each group opens on what set
  its constants.
- The "Fits beyond their range" paragraph runs PlaSim's radiation fits, PlaSim's cloud fits, then RRTMG's
  ranges, each sentence pairing a fit with the lunar range that exceeds it; the microphysical and surface laws
  follow in their own paragraph. Kept as a governed catalogue.
- The defects paragraph lists five defects, then lands on what hid them. Kept.

## 2. Inheritance

- Each bold-led paragraph opens on the form named in the opening paragraph's list; "Defects of the
  adaptation" opens on "The adaptation made defects of its own", inheriting the preceding paragraph's
  "defects"; the slice paragraph opens on "the inherited ones". Kept.
- **L15–17** "Its lightning scheme" took "Its" from "WRF-ELEC's NSSL scheme", which has no lightning scheme
  of its own. **Revised** to "Earth's air sets three thresholds in WRF-ELEC's lightning."
