\section{Availability, authorship and review}

\textbf{Code and data.} The patch series for ExoPlaSim, CM1 with WRF-ELEC and SWAN, the Fortran written for
CM1's electrified build, the design case's inputs, the in-house reference and calibration tools with their
tests, the notes on each component's correctness and limits, the defect log, the verification record, and
this manuscript's figure scripts, data extracts and claims register are held in the world-crafting
repository, whose release commit is recorded in the release register. The scenario, the runners, the case
set-up and the analyses stay in Terluna's repository at commit 186b294
\autocite{goldman2026terluna}. The upstream sources are pinned by checksum: ExoPlaSim 3.4.2 from its
source distribution, CM1 release 22.0, SWAN 41.51, SWASH 12.01 and WRF-ELEC at commit \code{e43041b}. The
patches inherit their upstream licences: the GNU GPL version 3 for ExoPlaSim and SWAN, CM1's MIT-style
licence, and the WRF public-domain notice for WRF-ELEC. The model runs themselves are held on the project's
research drive; the products derived from them are committed to Terluna with the hashes of their inputs.

\textbf{Author and assistance.} Daniel S. Goldman set the scenario and its questions, made the decisions
recorded in Terluna's decisions register and is responsible for the content. The adaptations, analyses and
this manuscript were developed with AI assistance. Claude Opus~5.5 (Anthropic), working under the author's
direction, wrote and ran most of the code, patches and analyses in Terluna, where 374 of the 487 commits to
186b294 name it as co-author,\claim{Z01} assembled the world-crafting repository and drafted this text. Every number
in the text is traced in the claims register to a file in one of the two repositories, a run on the research
drive or a stated calculation.

\textbf{Competing interests.} The author's declaration is pending.

\textbf{Review history.} The manuscript was drafted section by section on 17 October 2026. The reviews
performed on it, by whom and with what findings, are recorded in the repository's review folder; review by
a person other than the author, or by a journal, is still to come.

\textbf{Acknowledgements.} The work stands on models and data that their developers make freely available:
ExoPlaSim and the Planet Simulator, CM1, WRF-ELEC, SWAN and SWASH, the HITRAN database, the MT\_CKD
continuum and the TSIS-1 solar spectrum.
