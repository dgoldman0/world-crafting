\section{Availability and declarations}

\textbf{Code and data.} The ensemble's code and records are held in the public world-crafting repository,
\url{https://github.com/dgoldman0/world-crafting}, where work on the ensemble will continue as a standalone
project, if resources allow, and where this paper's release is tagged \code{paper-v1}. It holds the patches to
ExoPlaSim, CM1 with WRF-ELEC and SWAN, the Fortran written for CM1's electrified build, the in-house reference and
calibration tools with their tests, the notes on each component's correctness and limits, the defect log and the
verification record, with this paper's figure scripts, data extracts and a register tracing the work's own numbers
to their sources. The runs and their analyses belong to the Terluna project \autocite{goldman2026terluna}, whose public repository at commit 186b294, tagged \code{world-crafting-paper-v1}, holds the scenario, the runners that build and run the models, the case
set-up, the analyses, and the products derived from the runs with the hashes of their inputs; the raw model output
is available from the author on reasonable request. The patches apply to upstream sources pinned by checksum:
ExoPlaSim 3.4.2 from its source distribution, CM1 release 22.0, SWAN 41.51 and WRF-ELEC at commit \code{e43041b};
SWASH 12.01 runs unmodified from its pinned archive. As patches to those sources, they inherit their licences: the GNU GPL version 3 for ExoPlaSim and version 3 or later for SWAN, CM1's MIT-style
licence, and the WRF public-domain notice for WRF-ELEC.

\textbf{Author and AI assistance.} Daniel S. Goldman designed the scenario, directed the work and is responsible
for the content. The code, analyses and manuscript were developed with AI assistance: Claude Opus~5.5 (Anthropic)
wrote and ran most of the code and analyses\claim{Z01} and drafted most of the text, with subagents of the same
model checking its claims, sources and prose; OpenAI's Codex drafted the conclusion, revised and reviewed the whole
text and, in Terluna, reviewed research branches of the code; and the author's conversations with ChatGPT (OpenAI) were a source for the scenario's early planning and for the style rules applied to the text. The records of this assistance
are kept in the Terluna and world-crafting repositories, those of the manuscript's reviews in world-crafting.

\textbf{Competing interests.} The author declares no competing interests. The work is an unfunded personal project from which the author receives no income, and the author's practice is to make public every part of it that its
licences and sources allow.

\textbf{Acknowledgements.} The work used models and data that their developers make freely available: ExoPlaSim
and the Planet Simulator, CM1, WRF-ELEC, SWAN and SWASH, the HITRAN database, the MT\_CKD continuum and the
TSIS-1 solar spectrum.
