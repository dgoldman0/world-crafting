\section{Appendix: configurations, switches and checks}

The lunar runs' configurations, the changes with their switches and lunar settings, and the verification
record by kind of check, condensed from the code repository's record, are in Tables~\ref{tab:config},
\ref{tab:repairs} and \ref{tab:verification}.

\textbf{The curved-air table's channels.} Each channel is a group of a column's wavenumbers within bands 10--40~nm wide up to 1,250~nm and 50~cm$^{-1}$ wide beyond. Within each band the
wavenumbers are sorted by whole-column absorption and bisected by energy until each group, at its energy-weighted mean optical depths, absorbs a direct beam, on slant columns of 0.03 to 13 vertical columns, within 1\% of the group's
sunlight of what its members absorb; beyond 1,250~nm a group must also reproduce, within the same 1\%, the beam's loss
above the bottom of every layer, while below 500~nm each band is one channel. Of the 295~K column's 10,697 channels, the 282 below
1,250~nm whose whole-column absorption depth is under 3 are solved by spherical successive orders of scattering, continued
until the newest order adds less than $2\times10^{-5}$, with a cap of 100; the 301~K column's table has 11,559
channels, 267 of them solved so.\claim{R05}\claim{R24}
