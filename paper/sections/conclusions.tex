\section{Conclusions}

In the four codes examined, the form of an Earth value set the kind of repair it took. Of the fourteen general
repairs in Table~\ref{tab:inventory}, twelve correct a constant or a unit of time, and the other two extend fits,
the fall speeds by a similarity law and the cloud's optics by a parameterization fitted over a wider range; both
general methods correct geometry, computed again from each world's own column; and all four calibrations
correct fits and hold near the columns they were fitted on, while seven of the fifteen fits remain open. Three
of the five defects in the released codes produce little or no error at Earth's values and an error that grows
as a world departs from Earth in rotation, day length or tilt, and the same arithmetic biases ExoPlaSim's runs
of natural planets: the Sun clock skips back about 33\textdegree\ a model day in non-synchronous runs on the
default solar geometry at rotations slower than 20 days, the convective-cover unit raises the cover of
synchronous runs by about 0.23 at TRAPPIST-1e's rotation of 6.1 days, and the orbit default replaces a
configured tilt with Earth's unless the orbit is fixed. On a constructed world that departs from Earth along
several ratios at once, these errors grew large enough to find.

On the test world the parts fitted to one world's columns moved the climate furthest and the general repairs
least. Single general repairs moved the global-mean surface temperature by 1.6--2.5~K; the first calibration of
the radiation's Earth fits moved it by more than 20~K, the cloud's Earth-tuned settings, pushed to the ends of
their tests, by up to about 6~K, and the longwave correction by height, switched on with the ice optics, by
11.5~K in a state beyond its fit. The two atmospheric models disagree most where each computes with Earth fits of
its own: with the GCM's transport imposed, CM1's lowest 1.8~km at 45\textdegree\,N stays 3.7~K warmer than the
GCM's, and a box forced from the GCM brings a quarter of the forcing GCM's rain to the ground, while on tidally
locked planets cloud-permitting and parameterized models differ by a factor of two or more in cloud cover
\autocite{yang2023cloud}.

The worlds open to the adapted models are ordered by what a modeller supplies: nothing for the breathable Moon
near its fitted columns; the general repairs alone, by construction and as yet untested, for Earth-air planets of
a Sun-like star that differ from Earth in rotation; a column of the world's own and refitted calibrations for the
planets of M dwarfs, a breathable world on a small body or Earth-like air on a larger planet; and new radiation,
condensation and cloud for Titan, present Mars and Venus. A reduced correlated-$k$ scheme built from the
reference's spectroscopy in place of Sasamori's and Lacis and Hansen's fits would turn the largest calibrations
into a general repair, holding on any column whose gases and pressures that spectroscopy covers, while cloud
fitted to cloud-resolving runs would remain a calibration made for each world. Runs of the repaired ExoPlaSim on
Earth-air slow rotators would test the general repairs on the natural worlds they were built for.
