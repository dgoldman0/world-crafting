\begin{abstract}
\noindent Many of the general circulation models used for exoplanets were developed for Earth or for solar-system
planets, and on a world whose air and water resemble Earth's while its gravity, size, day or starlight differ, a
run that keeps a value set for Earth can complete with a plausible result. A constructed world can depart further:
by one design's own models, a shield in space that removes the ultraviolet heating the upper air keeps Earth-like
air under a gravity too weak to hold it naturally. Four Earth-built codes, ExoPlaSim~3.4.2, CM1 release~22 with
WRF-ELEC's electrification, SWAN~41.51 and SWASH~12.01, were adapted to such a world, a breathable Moon, which
departs from Earth at once in gravity, column mass, day and scale height in proportion to its radius. The Earth
values found in them take four forms (constants, units of time, geometry, and fits and simplifications beyond their
range), beside five defects in the released codes; three of the defects, exact or nearly so at Earth's values, bias
ExoPlaSim's runs of slowly rotating and tidally locked planets by the code's own arithmetic.\claim{H23}\claim{W06}
Constants and units of time took general repairs built to hold on other worlds, geometry a method computed for each
world's column, and the radiation's fits calibrations to an in-house line-by-line model that hold near the columns
they were fitted on. Identity, reproduction, arithmetic and gravity-similarity checks and Earth benchmarks test the
code, and the calibrations test it against the line-by-line model. Patched, SWAN reproduces a sixfold gravity
scaling of an Earth sea to 0.3\% in wave height, where the released code misses it by 5.4\%; the lunar climate is
unobserved.\claim{V04} On the test world the two general repairs measured singly moved the global-mean surface
temperature by 1.6--2.5~K, while the first calibration of the radiation's Earth fits moved it by at least 20~K and
the cloud's Earth-tuned settings, varied in tests, by up to about 6~K.\claim{G01}\claim{G14}\claim{G05} A radiation
scheme built from line-by-line spectroscopy would replace the largest of those fits with physics computed for each
world's air, within the gases, pressures and temperatures the spectroscopy covers, while cloud fitted to
cloud-resolving runs remains a calibration for each world.
\end{abstract}
