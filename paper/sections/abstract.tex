\begin{abstract}
\noindent Terluna's breathable Moon, 1.2~atm of nitrogen and oxygen under lunar gravity and a 29.53-day
solar day,\claim{S01} can be studied only through numerical models built and tuned on Earth. Carrying four of
them to the Moon, the ExoPlaSim~3.4.2 general circulation model, the CM1 release~22 cloud-resolving model
with WRF-ELEC's electrification grafted in, and the SWAN~41.51 and SWASH~12.01 wave models, exposed Earth
inside them in five forms: dimensional constants, formulas counted per day, flat and sunlit-only geometry,
empirical fits and simplifications used beyond their range, and latent defects. Among the defects,
ExoPlaSim's Sun clock skipped 32.5\textdegree\ each lunar model day,\claim{H23} its default orbit replaced
the configured tilt with Earth's, and SWAN read a breaking parameter uninitialised. The repairs fall into
two classes. General repairs, most of them parameters whose defaults reproduce the released code, apply
scaling laws such as fall speeds proportional to $g^{(b+1)/3}$, restore the curved geometry of an atmosphere
whose scale height is 3\% of the radius, add missing processes and remove defects; they carry to other
worlds within stated limits. Calibrations fit PlaSim's broadband radiation to an in-house line-by-line model
on the design case's columns, and hold near them. Identity runs, patch series that rebuild the deployed
sources behind the current results, gravity-similarity tests and Earth benchmarks verify the code, and the
lunar climate itself awaits a test. The adapted models place the climate's largest remaining uncertainty in
cloud amount, in the height at which the longwave cools the air, a correction worth about 11.5~K with the
ice optics,\claim{G05} and in the boundary layer, where CM1 and the GCM still differ by 3.7~K in the lowest
1.8~km at 45\textdegree\,N with the GCM's sideways transport imposed.\claim{G06} The general repairs should
extend to slowly rotating planets and to breathable small bodies; compositions, pressures and scale heights
beyond the adapted radiation and dynamics remain beyond reach.
\end{abstract}
